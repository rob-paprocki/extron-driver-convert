# IV-CAMA-UMB Installation

The IV-CAMA-UMB Universal Mounting Bracket can be mounted to the wall as a camera shelf mount or it can be mounted onto the ceiling for inverted camera configurations.

**NOTE**: Camera inversion is only available for IV-CAM-P12 Series and IV-CAM-P20 cameras.

Refer to the following sections for information on mounting the IV-CAMA-UMB.

## IV-CAMA-UMB Wall Installation

Use the following procedure to install the IV-CAMA-UMB to the wall:

1.  Choose a mounting location that is free of any wiring or other obstructions. Make a small hole in the surface near the center of the chosen location to verify that it is suitable for mounting.
    
2.  Use a level and masking tape to attach the included installation template to the surface at the chosen location. Ensure that the template is level before proceeding.
    
3.  Using the **Anchor Holes** locations on the template as a reference, drill two pilot holes into the mounting surface.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/NextGenCamera_Mounts/InstallUMBWallTemplate.png)
    
4.  Install two of the included drywall anchors into the pilot holes.
    
5.  If routing cables to the camera through the wall, make a suitable hole in the mounting surface.
    
6.  Detach the small plastic cable cover on the rear of the mount.
    
7.  Detach the large plastic cable cover from the top of the mount.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Plastic-Covers.png)
    
8.  Insert the two included M2.5 x 12 mm hand screws through the standoff holes on each side of the mount. 
    
    note: Do not tighten the screws into the camera; this is performed later in the procedure.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/NextGenCamera_Mounts/InstallUMB_FrontView.png)
    
9.  Route the cables through the rear cover hole.
    
10.  Screw two of the included 6.3 x 38 mm screws through the rear holes of the mount into the drywall anchors in the wall.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Wall-Screw.png)
    
11.  Connect all required cabling into the camera. For more information about the required cabling, refer to [Connect the Camera](Connect-the-Camera.htm).
    
12.  Attach the included release plate to the bottom of the camera. Then, tighten the included 1/4‑20 x 3/8 in. screw onto the release plate.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Add-Release-Plate.png)
    
13.  Slide the camera at a slight angle onto the mount so that the camera ports are facing the rear mounting holes.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Slide-Cam-In.png)
    
14.  Tighten the two included M2.5 x 12 mm hand screws into the rear standoff holes on the camera.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Thumb-Screws.png)
    
15.  Attach the small plastic cable cover into Position 1.
    
16.  Attach the large top cover to the top of the mount.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Plastic-Covers.png)
    

## IV-CAMA-UMB Ceiling Installation

Use the following procedure to install the IV-CAMA-UMB to the ceiling:

NOTE: Only install the IV-CAMA-UMB to a drywall or hard wall ceiling. If mounting to a ceiling tile, a 90 x 90 VESA mount is suggested.

1.  Choose a mounting location that is free of any wiring or other obstructions. Make a small hole in the surface near the center of the chosen location to verify that it is suitable for mounting.
    
2.  Use a level and masking tape to attach the included installation template to the surface at the chosen location. Ensure that the template is level before proceeding.
    
3.  Using the **Anchor Holes** locations on the template as a reference, drill four pilot holes into the mounting surface.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/NextGenCamera_Mounts/InstallUMBCeilingTemplate.png)
    
4.  If routing cables to the camera through the ceiling, make a suitable hole in the mounting surface.
    
5.  Attach the mount onto the ceiling using four screws and anchors (not included). Choose the mounting hardware length and type in accordance with building specifications.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Ceiling-Screw.png)
    
6.  Detach the small plastic cable cover on the rear of the mount.
    
7.  Detach the large plastic cable cover from the top of the mount.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Plastic-Covers.png)
    
8.  Route the cables through the bottom cover hole.
    
9.  Insert the two included M2.5 x 12 mm hand screws through the standoff holes on each side of the mount.
    
    note: Do not tighten the screws into the camera; this is performed later in the procedure.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/NextGenCamera_Mounts/InstallUMB_FrontView.png)
    
10.  Connect all required cabling into the camera. For more information about the required cabling, refer to [Connect the Camera](Connect-the-Camera.htm).
    
11.  Attach the included release plate to the bottom of the camera. Then, tighten the included 1/4‑20 x 3/8 in. screw onto the release plate.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Add-Release-Plate.png)
    
12.  Slide the camera at an angle onto the mount so that the camera ports are facing the rear mounting installation holes.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Slide-Cam-In.png)
    
13.  Tighten the two included M2.5 x 12 mm hand screws into the rear standoff holes on the camera.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Thumb-Screws.png)
    
14.  Attach the small plastic cable cover into Position 2.
    
15.  Attach the large top cover to the top of the mount.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/IV-CAMA_Plastic-Covers.png)
