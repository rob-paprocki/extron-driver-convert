# IV-CAM Series

Crestron 1 Beyond Cameras

The IV-CAM Series cameras are high quality PTZ cameras that output up to 1080p60 resolution video. Crestron 1 Beyond cameras are ideal for meetings in medium, large, and extra large spaces where one camera needs to capture several areas of the room. Intelligent features such as presenter tracking, group framing, and group tracking are available for IV-CAM-I12 and IV-CAM-I20 series cameras.
