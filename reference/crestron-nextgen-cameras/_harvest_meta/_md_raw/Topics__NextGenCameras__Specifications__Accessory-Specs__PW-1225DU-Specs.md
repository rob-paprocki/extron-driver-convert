# PW-1225DU Specifications

Product specifications for the PW-1225DU are provided below.

## Specifications

 

Output Rating

12VDC, 2.5A, regulated

Power Requirements

0.6A (maximum) @ 100-240VAC, 50/60 Hz

Connections

Line

(1) C8 2 pin main power input

Load

(1) 6 ft (1.83 m) attached cable with 5.5 x 3.3 mm DC power barrel connector

Environmental

Temperature

23° to 104°F (-5° to 40°C)

Humidity

5% to 90% RH (noncondensing)

Construction

Plastic, black, desktop

Weight

0.54 lb (0.24 kg)

Compliance

CE
