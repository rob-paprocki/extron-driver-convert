# IVA-WMT-BRKT-1B Installation

The IVA-WMT-BRKT-1B Wall Mount Bracket is used to mount Crestron 1 Beyond cameras to the wall when a camera tilt is required. The mounting plate attached to the camera can be tilted to center the camera shot.

To mount the camera to a wall using the IVA-WMT-BRKT-1B Wall Mount Bracket:

1.  Choose a mounting location that is free of any wiring, studs, or other obstructions. Make a small hole in the surface near the center of the chosen location to verify that it is suitable for mounting.
    
2.  Use a level and the wall mount as a template to position the pilot holes. Ensure that the wall mount is level before proceeding.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/WMTInstall_wmtinstall2.png)
    
3.  Using the four mounting holes on the mount, drill 4 pilot holes in the wall.
    
4.  If routing cables to the camera through the wall, make a suitable hole in the mounting surface.
    
5.  Insert the required cables for the camera through the rear of the mount to the open hole near the camera plate.
    
6.  Install four drywall anchors (not included) into the pilot holes.
    
7.  Attach the mount onto the wall using the four included screws.
    
8.  Use the included screw to fix the camera on the camera mount plate.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/WMTInstall_wmtinstall.png)
    
9.  Use a 4 mm Allen Key wrench to loosen the hinge bolt on the side of the camera mount.
    
    **NOTE:** If the camera plate is not centered horizontally (0° X-axis), the Allen Key pivot bolt underneath the camera plate can be used to correct the positioning.
    
    ![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Accessories/AppDiagrams/WMT_WMT-Bolts.png)
    
10.  Tilt the camera mount plate to achieve the desired camera tilt. For information on tilt angles for cameras, refer to [Mounting Measurements](Mounting-Requirements.htm#Mountingmeasure)
    
11.  Tighten the Allen Key bolt once the desired camera tilt is achieved.
