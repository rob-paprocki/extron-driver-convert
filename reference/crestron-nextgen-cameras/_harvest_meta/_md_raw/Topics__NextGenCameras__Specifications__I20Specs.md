# IV-CAM-I20 Specifications

Product specifications for the IV-CAM-I20 are provided below.

## Specifications

 

Optics

Image Sensor

1/2.8 Sony® CMOS Sensor

Optical Zoom

20x

Focal Length

F=5.2–94 mm

Field of View (PTZ)

Horizontal: 56.45°

Field of View (Reference)

Horizontal: 104° (changes based on camera mode)

Shutter Speed

1/50-1/10,000 sec

Iris

F1.5-F3.0

Minimum Illumination

2 lux

Pan/Tilt Angle

Pan: -130° — 130°, Tilt: -30° — 90°

Pan/Tilt Speed

Pan: 0.2° — 90°/s, Tilt: 0.2° — 70°/s

White Balance

Auto, Indoor, One Push, Manual

Exposure

Auto, Manual, Shutter Priority, Iris Priority, Brightness Priority

Signal to Noise Ratio

≥50dB

Camera Processing

Intelligent Video Functions

Presenter Tracking, Group Tracking, Preset Zones

Recommended Range

15–50 ft

Presets

Up to 256 configurable presets

Video

Video Outputs

3G-SDI, HDMI®, USB, NDI®|HX2, ONVIF, RTSP, UVC

Signal Formats

SDI or HDMI: 1080p60/59.94/50/30/  
29.94/25, 720p60/59.94/50;  
USB (NV12 and MJPEG): 1080p30/25, 720p30/25, 360p30

Communications

Ethernet

RJ-45, 100Mbps

Serial

RS-232, RS-485

Control Protocol

VISCA/TCP, VISCA/UDP, PELCO-D, PELCO-P, ONVIF

Management Client

Software

Crestron 1 Beyond Camera Manager 2 (Windows® OS computer required)

Connectors

COM

(1) 5-pin 3.5 mm detachable terminal block;  
Bidirectional RS-232/485 port

PoE

(1) 8-pin RJ-45 connector, female

3G-SDI OUT

(1) BNC connector, female;  
3G-SDI digital video output

HDMI OUT

(1) HDMI Type A connector;  
HDMI digital video output

USB

(1) USB Type B connector, female;  
USB 3.0 device port for video and control;  
USB 2.0 device port for video (MJPEG only) and control

12VDC 2.5A

(1) 3.3 mm DC power connector;  
12VDC power input;  
[PW-1225DU](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513216 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513216") power pack sold separately  

Controls and Indicators

Status Bar

(1) Multisegment tricolor LED bargraph;  
Half green LED bargraph indicates live camera feed;  
Full green LED bargraph indicates camera tracking;  
Full amber LED bargraph indicates firmware upgrade;  
Half red LED bargraph indicates Privacy Mode  

RESET

(1) Recessed push button for hardware reset

Power

Power Source Options

Ethernet (PoE), Power Pack ([PW-1225DU](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513216 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513216"))

Power over Ethernet

IEEE 802.3af Class 3 Powered Device

Power over Ethernet Rating

15.4 W

NOTE: To comply with the European Directive (CE), shielded CAT5e cable must be used as a minimum for PoE power.

Power Pack (sold separately)

12VDC, 2.5A, 50/60 Hz

Environmental

Temperature

32° to 104°F (0° to 40°C)

Humidity

10% to 90% RH (noncondensing)

Construction

Mounting Options

[IVA‑WMT‑BRKT‑1B](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513408 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513408") 1 Beyond Wall Mount Bracket (included), [IVA‑CMT‑BRKTJ‑1B](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513409 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513409") 1 Beyond J‑Mount Bracket (sold separately), [IVA‑CAMA‑UMB](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513214 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513214") 1 Beyond Universal Mounting Bracket (sold separately)

Mounting Thread

1/4 in. threaded mount hole

Dimensions

Height

6.51 in. (165 mm)  

Width

8.90 in. (226 mm)

Depth

5.86 in. (149 mm)  

Weight

4.11 lb (1.86 kg)

Compliance

**Regulatory Model: M202306003**  

FCC Part 15 Class B, IC Class B, CE, Intertek® Listed for US and Canada
