# IV-CAM-I12-GV-B Features

The [IV-CAM-I12-GV-B](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513987 "https://www.crestron.com/model/6513987") is a high quality intelligent PTZ camera that uses visual AI to automatically track or frame a presenter. The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B is ideal for group framing meeting participants in medium to large spaces, and presenter tracking in medium sized presentation areas. The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B has a dual camera design; the wide‑angle reference camera uses visual AI to direct the PTZ camera. All visual AI is built into the camera—no external system is needed.

The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B is a TAA-compliant camera that is suitable for government deployments.

![](../../../Resources/Images/SS-Cameras/IV-CAM-I12-B-FrontRightt30.png)

*   TAA compliant for government deployments
*   Visual AI for Group Framing or Presenter Tracking
*   High quality video supporting resolutions up to 1080p60
*   Automatically pans, tilts, and zooms with dual camera technology, no external computer required
*   Supports 12x optical zoom for tracking distances up to 25 ft from participants
*   Connects directly to a codec or recording/streaming device
*   Single Ethernet connection provides power (PoE), viewing, and control
*   Easily configured with Crestron 1 Beyond Camera Manager software
*   Compatible with Automate VX multi-camera systems
*   Compatible with Crestron 1 Beyond IR Camera Remote

#### Group Framing Technology

Group framing utilizes visual AI to frame multiple participants smoothly in medium to large conference rooms. The wide‑angle reference camera tracks participant movement, and the PTZ camera automatically reframes to keep them in view.

#### Presenter Tracking Capability

Visual AI is used to smoothly track a single moving presenter as they move through a designated presentation area. It is easily configurable to prevent tracking other subjects or displays by using Blocking Zones. The recommended tracking range is 10 to 25 ft from the presentation area.

#### Multi-Camera Switching Capability

Two Crestron intelligent cameras can be set up to utilize intelligent switching. One camera can be designated as a group framing camera ([IV‑CAM‑I12](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513204 "https://www.crestron.com/model/6513204")), and the other can be used as a presenter tracking camera ([IV‑CAM‑I12](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513204 "https://www.crestron.com/model/6513204") or [IV‑CAM‑I20](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513200 "https://www.crestron.com/model/6513200")). This feature allows for seamless transition between the group framing camera and the presenter tracking camera when someone enters the presentation zone—no additional hardware or software required.

#### Automate VX Compatible

The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B camera is compatible with Automate VX multi-camera speaker tracking systems. Use the IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B for close up shots of active speaking participants, and for presenter tracking in a designated area within 25 ft.

#### Certified for Conferencing Software

The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B camera is certified for Microsoft Teams® Rooms and Zoom Rooms® conferencing software. Use the rear USB port and connect a USB cable to a compatible codec for conferencing software use.

#### High Quality Video

A high quality Sony® CMOS sensor enables the camera to output up to 1080p60 resolution video.

#### Networked Power, Viewing, and Control

Connect a single Ethernet cable to power (PoE), view, set up, and control the IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B camera.

#### Mounting Options (Sold Separately)

Standard mounting holes make it easy to mount the camera. The IV‑CAM‑I12‑GV‑B is compatible with the [IV‑CAMA‑UMB](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513214 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513214") Universal Mounting Bracket, [IVA‑WMT‑BRKT‑1B](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513408 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513408") Wall Mount Bracket, and [IVA‑CMT‑BRKT‑1B](https://www.crestron.com/model/6513409 "https://www.Crestron.com/model/6513409") J‑Mount Ceiling Bracket.
