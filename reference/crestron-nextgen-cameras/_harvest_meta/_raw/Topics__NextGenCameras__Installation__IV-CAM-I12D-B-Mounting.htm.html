IV-CAM-I12D-B Installation

The IV-CAM-I12D-B Crestron 1 Beyond i12D Camera can be mounted to the wall as a camera shelf mount or it can be placed on a tabletop or similar flat surface.

Refer to the following sections for more information on mounting the IV-CAM-I12D-B.

IV-CAM-I12D-B Installation Requirements

The IV-CAM-I12D-B camera has additional installation requirements:

  • The IV-CAM-I12D-B camera cannot be inverted.

  • The fixed wide-angle camera lens must be able to view the entire subject area as it is used by the tracking algorithm for visual AI.
  • Ensure that the camera is at least 5 ft away from the closest meeting participant.
  • Ensure that the camera is within 25 ft of the furthest meeting participant.
  • The IV-CAM-I12D-B camera must be mounted 4 to 7 ft (1.8 to 2.1 m) from the ground.

IV-CAM-I12D-B Tabletop Installation

The IV-CAM-I12D-B can be placed onto a tabletop or similar flat surface. When surface mounting the IV‑CAM‑I12D‑B, ensure it is placed as close as possible to the front edge of the surface. Poor speaker tracking detection due to inaccurate spacial audio may occur if the camera is placed too far back on the surface.

IV-CAM-I12D-B Wall Installation

To install the IV-CAM-I12D-B to the wall with the included wall mount:

IV-CAM-I12D-B Mount

  1. Choose a mounting location that is free of any wiring or other obstructions. Make a small hole in the surface near the center of the chosen location to verify that it is suitable for mounting.

  2. Use a level and the wall mount as a template to mark four pilot holes. Ensure the pilot holes are aligned with the four wall mount holes and that the mount is level before proceeding.

  3. Drill four pilot holes into the mounting surface using the locations marked in step 2.

  4. If routing cables to the camera through the wall, make a suitable hole in the mounting surface.

  5. Insert the required cables for the camera through the rear of the mount to the open hole near the camera plate.

  6. Install four drywall anchors (not included) into the pilot holes.

  7. Attach the mount onto the wall using the four included M6*40 screws.

  8. Align the three threaded screw holes in the bottom of the IV-CAM-I12D-B with the three bottom plate holes in the wall mount.

  9. Attach the IV-CAM-I12D-B to the mount using the three included 1/4-20UNC x 8mm screws.
