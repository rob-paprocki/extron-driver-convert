# Roadmap

*Written 2026-09-11 from a deduplicated backlog. Every status claim below was re-checked against the working tree that day, then corrected for the commits that landed while it was being written. **Refreshed 2026-09-23** for the processor sessions of 09-13 to 09-18 and for the offline work since. Effort: **S** is under half a day, **M** is 1–3 days, **L** is a week or more.*

All four conversion directions work offline, and the standard-library suites pass (the count lives in `ENVIRONMENT.md`). The i20 driver exists in two forms:
- **`.pkp`:** `1bynd_19_20028` (v1.6), 45 commands on the IV-CAM-I20 and 42 on the IV-CAM-I12, with parity with Crestron's own I20 driver (2026-09-23). Its predecessors `20025` and `20026` built and uploaded from GC Pro to an IPCP Pro 360, were controlled from a TLP Pro 725M with a PC playing the camera, and the panel's feedback followed the camera in both directions (`experiments/skeleton_i20/PROTOCOL.md` run log, 2026-09-14 to 09-18). Extron's `LoadFromFile` accepts `20028`; it awaits the same upload.
- **ControlScript module:** 43 commands, identical wire bytes. **It has never run on a processor** (H1, H1L Path A).

**"Done" today means the `.pkp` runs on a processor against a simulated camera.** No socket has ever been opened to a real camera, so every reply parser still rests on documentation. The next milestone is a camera that moves under an Extron processor. The GCP licence is not a deadline: it renews for 30 days whenever GCP starts online and checks in with Extron (§6).

**The 2026-09 pack-up is done and closed.** Everything a retired workstation held is either in this repo or, where it carries account or licence data, in the git-ignored `private/`. *(Since 2026-09-24 the vendor material among it is on disk but untracked, pinned by `vendor-files.manifest.tsv` — D1.)* All 195 project source files were matched byte-for-byte and the 6,354 corpus files SHA-256 verified. `notes/2026-09-pack-up.md` records what moved where; it is history and needs nothing further.
